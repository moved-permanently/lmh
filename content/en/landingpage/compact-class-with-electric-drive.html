Maximum Variety, the Perfect Fit for Any Application

In the payload class of 1 to 2 metric tons, Linde MH’s electric forklift trucks offer a unique range of models with 40 different configurations. Thanks to the wide selection of battery capacities, load capacities, chassis sizes, and steering axles, there is a perfect setup for every application. The E14 – E20 models impress with maximum flexibility in power sources and equipment, making them suitable for numerous application scenarios. For standard applications without battery changes, the Ei14 – Ei20 models with permanently integrated Li-ion batteries are the ideal choice. Unbeatable performance is guaranteed by the Xi10 – Xi20: Their 90-volt technology with synchronous reluctance motors ensures full power even during continuous operation. In addition, Linde MH offers the Roadster models Xi14 – Xi20 R , a special variant optimized for maximum all-around visibility.

​

Style
headline, spacer

Impressive multi-talent: E14 – E20

The E14 – E20 are the ideal solution for tight environments in warehouses or production facilities. They combine a compact design and outstanding maneuverability with a top speed of up to 20 km/h. The E14 – E20 rely on a powerful 48 V electrical system that delivers an optimal ratio of performance, energy efficiency, and cost-effectiveness. There are a total of 14 different model variants to choose from, which differ in terms of their load capacity, battery size, height, and whether they are designed as a three-wheeled or four-wheeled version .

​

Ultimate flexibility also applies to the energy source: fleet operators can choose between the lead-acid batteries, Li-ION batteriesand fuel cell systems offered by Linde Material Handling. Thanks to the plug & play principle, they can continue to use existing batteries or switch between lead-acid and Li-ION batteries without having to convert. The side battery door also makes it easier to change the battery.

​

The Master Performer: Xi10 – Xi20

The Xi10 – Xi20 have been shown to be the highest-performing representatives of their payload class. In combination with a 90 V Li-ION battery and sophisticated heat management, they increase the efficiency of the drive and lift motors by up to 5 percent and thus guarantee full performance at all times, even in continuous operation. With lifting and lowering speeds around 20 percent above the industry average, the Xi10 - Xi20 set new standards in productivity.

A range of battery capacities up to 32 kWh enables the forklifts to be flexibly adapted to any operating conditions. Thanks to their robust design, they can also be used outdoors in the four-wheeled version with pendulum axle (Xi16 – Xi20 P only, three-wheeled version Xi10 – Xi20) without having to make any compromises. Altogether, the Xi10 – Xi20 are available in 12 different design variants.

Straightforward indoor heroes: Ei14 – Ei20

The electric counterbalanced forklifts Ei14 – Ei20 from Linde Material Handling are based on the proven drive technology of the E-series models while offering the convenience of the Xi series thanks to their integrated Li-ion battery. Quick opportunity charging – optionally via an onboard charger – is possible during the shift or during breaks.

This makes the forklifts ideal for applications that do not require battery changes and also offers a cost-effective alternative for two-shift operations that have previously relied on lead-acid exchange batteries. With speeds of up to 20 km/h, they handle demanding transport tasks efficiently and reliably. Maximum maneuverability is guaranteed by the turntable steering on the three-wheel model and the Linde combi steering axle on the four-wheel model.

The Spacious Miracle

The permanently integrated Li-ION battery in the Xi10 – Xi20 and Ei14 – Ei20 models, and the absence of a battery tray have allowed the workplace to be redesigned. In addition to the increased headroom, the footwell in particular has been significantly expanded and now offers the driver legroom that is a significant 35 percent greater than the industry average. The step was enlarged and lowered at the same time, so that it is now just 40 centimeters above the ground. This means that the Xi10 – Xi20 and Ei14 – Ei20 offer the lowest entry step in the industry and guarantee maximum comfort and safety when getting in and out of the truck.

​

The renowned trade journalist and forklift truck expert Theo Egberts carried out an extensive performance test, which confirmed the outstanding performance, speed, and maneuverability of the Xi models. These results reinforce the status of the E14 – E20 and Xi10 – Xi20 as the world’s most powerful electric forklift trucks in their class and confirm that they are setting new standards for payloads up to two metric tons.

Maximum All-Round Visibility: The Roadsters

The Roadsters are special versions of the Xi series. Xi14 – Xi20 R and Xi16 – Xi20 RP. They offer a unique combination of unrestricted all-round visibility, high performance, and ergonomic design. The distinctive Linde design features overhead tilt cylinders, removing the need for an A-pillar and providing an unobstructed view to the front of the truck. This gives the driver a clear view of their route, load, and the surrounding environment. The Roadsters are particularly well suited for use in confined spaces and for loading and unloading trucks via a ramp. The impressive Xi14 – Xi20 R are agile 3-wheel models featuring a pivot-steer axle, while the Xi16 – Xi20 RP are 4-wheel models that offer maximum stability and maneuverability. With the optional Linde Steer Control , the Roadster can be controlled by joystick or mini steering wheel, significantly reducing the physical strain on the driver. What’s more, the elimination of the steering column also improves visibility.

Three-wheeled forklifts from Linde MH use the tried-and-tested swivel axle. The benefits of this axle variant come to the fore when it is used indoors. It performs particularly well in transport operations in tight spaces where maximum maneuverability is required.

Ergonomics: Comfort for Maximum Productivity

The driver’s wellbeing has a decisive impact on their productivity. It is for that reason that the compact class offers a level of comfort and user-friendliness previously only seen in IC trucks. The mast, drive axle, and steering axle are decoupled from the chassis by elastomer ring bearings to ensure stress-free work. This improves shock absorption and protects the driver when driving over uneven surfaces.

The ergonomic layout of the control elements, the spacious cab with improved cooling and heating performance, and the intuitive operation also help to minimize the physical strain on the driver. The driver’s cab has been kept in the same automotive design. The cabs of the E14 – E20, Ei14 – Ei20 and Xi10 – Xi20 feature equipment such as a 3.5-inch full-graphic display. The spacious headroom in the driver’s cab helps to provide additional comfort.

More on ergonomics in Linde forklifts

Safety: Top Protection in Every Situation

Protection of the driver and the goods being transported is the top priority for the E14 – E20, Ei14 – Ei20 and Xi10 – Xi20 . Design elements and intelligent assistance systems ensure optimum protection of the driver, the load, and the environment. The slim mast profiles, the narrow A-pillar, and the low front plate ensure excellent all-round visibility. Thanks to the special design of the Xi14 – Xi20 R and Xi16 – Xi20 RP Roadster variants, the A-pillar has been removed entirely, further enhancing the driver’s field of vision. A strut-free roof pane made of armored glass is available as an optional extra for all models, providing a clear view when accessing high shelving.

The low center of gravity, the overhead tilt cylinders, and the torsion-resistant mast construction ensure maximum tilt safety. The forklift trucks are equipped as standard with Linde Curve Assist for automatic speed adjustment when cornering and Linde Load Assist to limit mast inclination and lifting movement. Additional optional assistance systems such as Linde Safety Pilot or Linde Reverse Assist Radar help to further increase safety.

More on safety in Linde forklifts

Connectivity: Seamless Integration and Maximum Availability

The E14 – E20, Ei14 – Ei20 and Xi10 – Xi20 can be seamlessly integrated into digital processes. Thanks to their modern electrical/electronic system architecture and sophisticated sensors, the trucks can be easily connected to Warehouse management systems, control systems, fleet management and service applications. There is also an optional (E14 – E20, Ei14 – Ei20) or standard (all Xi models) data transmission unit available, which enables the wireless transmission of key truck data, such as operating hours or error codes.

Recording error codes helps to ensure any wear or damage is detected at an early stage, which guarantees high truck availability. Software updates and new functions can be installed remotely without the need for a service technician on site. At the same time, fleet managers and service technicians can access and analyze important parameters and performance data at any time.

Service: Robust and Low-Maintenance for Minimal Costs

Their tough construction means that the compact class with electric drive is designed for high availability and low service costs. The drive axle, electric motors, and power inverter are fully encapsulated and are therefore protected from dust and water, and are completely maintenance-free. All components that require servicing are easily accessible to technicians, which speeds up maintenance work.

A hinged battery cover also simplifies the daily inspection of the lead-acid battery (E14 – E20 only). The wireless transmission of truck data helps to optimize service processes and avoid unnecessary downtime. An overall inspection is only required after 1,000 operating hours, and the change of hydraulic oil and hydraulic oil filter is only required after three years (about 6,000 hours).
