ERGONOMICS & OPERATOR COMFORT

Everything begins with the right posture.

A fully decoupled driver´s workstation absorbs vibrations before they reach the driver. Thanks to fully adjustable components – from the pedal plate and steering to the seat and display – the workstation adapts perfectly to every driver. Wide entry, generous legroom, and integrated padding keep every shift comfortable.

HANDLING & SAFETY

Full control. At all times.

Linde Load Control delivers centimeter-precise mast operation through two integrated armrest levers. Thanks to the standard load wheel brakes, braking force is distributed across all wheels, not just the drive wheels. This results in shorter stopping distances – a genuine differentiator. Linde Curve Assist and optional Rack Protection Sensor add further layers of safety.

CONNECTIVITY, SUSTAINABILITY & MAINTENANCE

Built for the long run.

The integrated 14.3 KWh li-ION battery is maintenance-free – and can be replaced with a 21.4 kWh unit when demands grow. Durable components reduce service effort, while the modern E&E architecture enables remote diagnosis and updates via the optional data transfer unit.

PERFORMANCE & EFFICIENCY

Compact form. Precise movement. Pure agility.

The integrated battery enables a more compact design and an excellent turning radius – agility when it counts. Optional PowerLift and PowerDrive increase lifting and travel speeds. An optional exchange device for the Li-ion battery can ensure the truck remains operational even in more demanding multi-shift applications.
