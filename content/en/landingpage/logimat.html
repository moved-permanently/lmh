Linde Xi Roadster – maximum visibility, maximum control

​

​

The Linde Xi Roadster attracted a lot of attention at LogiMAT: It offers an exceptionally unobstructed field of vision—made possible by the elimination of the A-pillar. The result: precise, fast, and safe operation, both in narrow racking areas and in demanding outdoor applications.

​

The robust construction, ergonomic operating concept, and modern energy options create a driving experience that impresses—from the first to the last use.

​

Highlights:

​

​

  • All-round visibility for fast, controlled work
  • Ergonomic operating concept for high driver comfort
  • Attractive total cost of ownership thanks to modern energy options

​

Reach trucks Ri14 – Ri18 – compact, connected, efficient

​

​

The new Ri14 to Ri18 generation of reach trucks showed how powerful compact warehouse technology is today. Load capacities from 1.4 to 1.8 tons, a compact chassis, lithium-ion battery, and a networked vehicle architecture ensure reliable performance, even in very narrow aisles.

​

Highlights:

​

​

  • Compact L2 dimension for narrow aisles
  • Excellent all-round visibility
  • Standard connectivityfor operational and diagnostic data
