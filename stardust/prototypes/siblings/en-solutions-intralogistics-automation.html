    Intralogistics and Automation

    Composed Around You
    Want to boost your operations? Get in touch with us!

    Goods handling in intralogistics is changing rapidly. Volumes and requirements are increasing, and processes are becoming more complex. The shortage of skilled workers is adding to this pressure. Companies therefore need to organize their material and goods flows in a flexible, efficient, and reliable way, while keeping an eye on their costs. There is a growing demand for intralogistics solutions that can be seamlessly integrated into value chains and adapted to changing conditions.

    This is exactly where Linde Material Handling comes in.

    With the digitalization and automation of intralogistics—using software-controlled guided vehicles, conveyor technology, and storage systems, with intelligent warehouse management to optimize all goods flows and processes. System solutions that increase efficiency, reduce costs, minimize errors and are tailored to the individual requirements of your company—Composed Around You.

    Advantages of Digitalization and Automation

    There are many ways to improve the performance of your intralogistics. These include optimizing vehicle fleets, storage areas, or warehouse layouts, and designing efficient goods flows. However, two of the most important levers are digitalization and automation. Together, they ensure transparency and efficiency throughout the entire system.

    • Digitalization lays the groundwork for efficient processes by connecting data and material flows, thereby creating transparency and controllability.
    • Automation builds on this foundation to optimize processes, conserve resources, and enhance the performance of intralogistics.

    Increase throughput

    More efficient throughput allows you to move larger quantities of goods in less time and make optimal use of your existing resources.

    Optimize space

    Stationary automated systems reduce space requirements as they enable greater storage capacity in the same space.

    Reduce costs

    Automation permanently reduces intralogistics costs by minimizing errors and optimizing the reliability of your processes.

    Ensure transparency

    Comprehensive data enables targeted performance analyses and shows you potential areas for improvement.

    Increase safety

    Automated processes reduce damage and accidents, thereby increasing safety throughout the entire operation.

    Relieve employees

    Automated processes relieve your employees so that they can concentrate on value-adding tasks.

    Decades of intralogistics experience in all relevant industries and over 1,400 automated systems in operation make Linde MH a strong partner for any task. Close cooperation with leading suppliers ensures tailor-made solutions.

    Linde Material Handling provides everything you need to optimize your intralogistics and successfully automate your processes.     Consulting

    Analysis and Target Vision

    Based on your objectives and current processes, we work with you to develop the optimal target state for your intralogistics. We use existing data and, if necessary, collect our own data on site to record and analyze all material flows and requirements along the handling processes. This enables us to determine the individual optimization and automation potential for your operational processes.

    Concept and Planning

    Based on an analysis of the current situation and your objectives, we develop a concept that combines material flow planning, fleet optimization, warehouse layout, and suitable automation components. Simulation and visualization help to verify the optimal future setup. Transparent cost and implementation planning with a view to ROI and TCO shows when your investment will pay off and what added value it will create in the long term.

    Employee training

    Reliability and service

    Thanks to careful planning, we consistently implement projects within the agreed time frame and budget. We also provide our customers with particularly close support during the start-up phase after commissioning. Enhanced service support ensures that operations run smoothly from day one.

    Products from Linde MH

    Our Portfolio: the Right Solution for Every Need

    Linde MH’s automation solutions are as diverse as our customers’ requirements. With our extensive experience in mobile applications, a high level of expertise in automating existing systems, and well-designed solutions for new construction projects, we offer the right product selection for every need.

    These include automated Linde industrial trucks (AGVs), autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), and configurable AGVs. Where high throughput and compaction are possible, we use stationary solutions, such as automated pallet shuttle systems, container storage systems, conveyor technology, and storage and retrieval machines.

    We combine this wide variety of mobile and stationary systems flexibly to create individual scenarios and cover all processes, from incoming to outgoing goods.

    Mobile systems

    Autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and automated guided vehicles (AGVs) ensure flexible and efficient handling and transport of a wide variety of goods and load carriers.

    Stationary systems

    Shuttle systems, compact warehouses, automated small parts warehouses, and storage and retrieval machines enable optimal use of space and high throughput in material flow.

    Additional components

    Modern conveyor technology, ergonomic picking stations, and racking systems from proven Linde partners round off the complete range of our automation solutions.

    One-stop warehouse automation: Discover our portfolio!

    Systems that Grow with You

    Automation solutions from Linde MH can be quickly integrated, flexibly expanded, and seamlessly adapted to existing processes. You can start with just one to five vehicles. From there, you can gradually expand and adapt the system to your business’s growing automation needs, all the way to a fully automated system.

    Flexible start

    Thanks to the Linde MATIC:move control software, your automation can start with individual process steps and just a few vehicles, even without a WMS connection. With digital mapping and visualization of the routes in the control software, you can use the AGVs safely and efficiently. Your employees can easily add routes or stations themselves at any time.

    On-site feasibility test

    Automated guided vehicles (AGVs) and autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) can be tested on site in trial operation. This allows the feasibility to be realistically assessed within a day. Once the tasks for AGVs or AMRs have been defined after successful testing and the integration of the devices into existing processes has been designed, commissioning usually takes place within three days.

    Seamless integration

    Whether for new construction projects or existing facilities, all components (from mobile and stationary systems to the control software Linde MATIC:move and MATIC:move+) are optimally coordinated and can be easily integrated into existing processes and IT systems thanks to the VDA 5050 interface.

    Maximum Flexibility

    The control software Linde MATIC:move+ for Linde MH’s AGVs is flexibly scalable and can be used with our WMS Linde Warehouse Manager , or can also be easily integrated into other higher-level warehouse management systems via the standard VDA 5050 interface. The Linde Warehouse Manager coordinates the interaction between manual and automated systems.

    Continuous development

    We are constantly expanding our product range and adapting it to new requirements. By working with technology partners and leveraging the resources of the KION Group, we can offer our customers modern, high-performance, and future-proof solutions.

    Software for Intralogistics and Automation

    Efficiency through Digitalization

    Whether manual or automated, the use of software systems is a prerequisite for efficient intralogistics processes. Linde MH’s warehouse management system (WMS), Linde Warehouse Manager , links the data flows of the supply chain with the physical processes in the warehouse. This allows all processes to be optimally controlled, monitored, and analyzed.

    Learn more about the Linde Warehouse Manager!

    Central control of all processes

    The Linde WMS controls both manual and automated processes and coordinates all material flows in the warehouse, from storage and picking to shipping.

    Transparency and inventory management

    Inventories as well as incoming and outgoing goods are fully recorded and managed. All processes are clearly documented and can be traced at any time.

    Networking with other systems

    Interfaces can be used to connect the forklift guidance system, the integrated forklift guidance system for manual vehicles, and the control software for automated systems. Integration into the company’s ERP system is also possible.

    Open interfaces and system diversity

    The Linde WMS is VDA 5050-compatible, allowing automated guided vehicles and control software from different manufacturers to communicate with each other. The solution is compatible with all common WMS systems.
