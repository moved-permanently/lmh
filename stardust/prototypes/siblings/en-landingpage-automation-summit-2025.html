    Thank You for an Impressive Linde Automation Summit 2025!

    What an incredible two days at our Linde Experience Hub in Aschaffenburg! We were thrilled to welcome international automation experts, forward-thinking customers, leading press members, and intralogistics multipliers/consultants to the Linde Automation Summit 2025.

    Together, we explored insightful industry talks, showcased our comprehensive solution portfolio, and provided a firsthand experience of the future of intralogistics with our latest AI-driven processes.

    Ready to optimize your operations? The conversation doesn't end here. If you're ready to discuss specific solutions for your business, contact our experts today.

    Let´s get in touch!

    Relive the Highlights: Browse Our Exclusive Photo and Video Gallery.

    1 / 26

    Highlights at a Glance

    • Linde Automated Solutions in Action: Attendees witnessed live demonstrations of our advanced automated solutions, showcasing their efficiency and precision.
    • AI Insights: The event featured a deep dive into the future of warehousing with Linde Material Handling and NVIDIA.
    • Next-Gen Tech: We were proud to offer a first look at the new automated Linde counterbalance truck and share more innovations to come.
    • Software Solutions: Live demos provided key insights into our scalable, easy-to-use automation software.
    • Expert Talks & Success Stories: Sessions covered the latest trends in automation and featured best practices from our valued customers.
    • Networking: The summit served as a key forum for automation and intralogistics experts to connect with their peers and share ideas.

    Agenda of the Automation Summit

    Two days, two powerful networks.

    Customer Day – November 12, 2025

    The Customer Day was designed for automation decision-makers (C-Level, Directors, Department Heads, Senior Engineers) from manufacturing and other industries with automation potential.

    See Agenda Customer Day

    Press & Consultants Day – November 13, 2025

    The Press & Consultants Day was exclusively crafted for esteemed key press representatives and intralogistics consultants who shape insights and narratives within our industry.

    See Agenda Press & Consultants Day

    Speakers

    Our lineup of industry experts shared firsthand knowledge from the front lines of intralogistics, detailing what works, what doesn’t, and how to apply proven solutions to today's operational challenges.

    Dr. Oliver Zuchowski is a Founder, Consultant and Speaker.

    As Head of Intralogistics Automation at Bosch, he leads the global strategy for mobile robotics. Leveraging over 10 years of experience, he helps companies successfully realize their automation plans.

    Matthias Koblitz has been shaping industrial automation for years, from DB Schenker to Bosch.

    As Senior Automation Consultant and Product Owner at Bosch, he helps plants worldwide unlock the value of mobile robotics, from technology selection to ROI-driven rollouts and sustainable scaling.

    Hendrik van Duuren – ROSSMANN From a strategic logistician in the Royal Netherlands Army to managing international supply chains at Herbalife,

    and ultimately to the pinnacle of retail logistics: Today, as Head of Logistics for Dirk Rossmann GmbH, Hendrik van Duuren oversees one of the most demanding logistics operations in the German retail industry.

    Luca Borg – ROSSMANN With a Master's degree in Production and Logistics Management, Luca drives innovative projects at ROSSMANN.

    He most recently managed the logistics for the permanent display, which won the ECR Award last year. He is now the project lead for the major innovative project, RoCaP.

    Matthias Baier As Director Supply Chain Management Industrial Technology at ZF Friedrichshafen AG, Matthias Baier drives strategic development and efficient global supply chain solutions.

    With a rich career trajectory at ZF, including leadership roles in logistics and supply chain design, he leverages extensive expertise to enhance competitive advantages and strengthen supply chain resilience in industrial technology.

    Tobias Arndt is Executive Vice President Global Operations at SCHUNK SE & Co. KG, where he oversees the entire process from order fulfillment to customer delivery.

    With over 20 years of experience in logistics and supply chain management, he possesses comprehensive expertise and a deep understanding of the demands of modern supply chains.

    Your Hosts

    Ulrike Just has been Executive Vice President Sales & Service for Linde Material Handling in EMEA since 2023 and a member of the Executive Board of Linde Material Handling GmbH since 2024.

    Frank Heptner, Vice President and Co-Lead of the Automation Business Unit of Linde Material Handling, brings over 25 years of extensive experience in supply chain solutions and international sales. A seasoned expert in developing sales structures and driving change management, Frank specializes in international sales and realization of holistic and integrated automated warehouse solutions.
