    FIND THE RIGHT SOUND WITH OUR AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS.

    Mobile and stationary automation form the instruments in the mix of modern intralogistics. Proven AGVs as well as storage and transportation solutions are designed to interact seamlessly – versatile, flexible and tuned to a perfect integrated solution. This creates a harmonious material flow that makes your automation solution the steady beat of long-term efficiency.

    PROVEN TECHNOLOGY

    Based on our proven technology, our mix of mobile and stationary automation solutions is robust, reliable and performance-driven.​

    • ​One of the broadest portfolios on the market
    • Modular, scalable and customizable
    • Industrial-scale production with the flexibility of bespoke engineering
    • Deeply embedded safety expertise with redundant systems and intelligent sensors
    • Seamless integration of manual and automated fleets as well as storage systems

    ​The result: sustainable performance and true one-stop solutions.

    MIXING IT ALL TOGETHER​

    Like a mixing console that unites all channels, our software solutions ensure seamless integration: processes, mobile and stationary automation or manual fleets interact and ensure that all systems play together in perfect sync. Scalable, secure and adaptable at any time – for full control and maximum transparency in real time.​

    SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS

    Our software connects all layers to guarantee seamless integration and reliable intralogistics.​

    • ​Quick start: Fast implementation of our AGV solution portfolio with our control software MATIC:move​
    • Grow: Add more complexity and scale with MATIC:move+ as your business evolves​
    • Flexible connection: Use your individual warehouse systems (WMS, SAP) or our performant inhouse WMS Linde Warehouse Manager​ to ensure your material flow
    • Open interfaces and VDA-5050 compliance for interoperability and future-proof investment​
    • Certified data protection and cybersecurity for maximum trust and transparency​

    ​This creates a holistic system that grows with your business, delivering measurable efficiency and long-term success.
