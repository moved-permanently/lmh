    It’s impossible to overstate the importance of safety in intralogistics. In addition to substantial costs, accidents and damage can have other serious consequences. Accidents involving people in particular often lead to personal harm and lengthy recovery processes. This can also impact productivity and the smooth running of operations, which can have extremely negative knock-on effects for the company’s competitiveness.

    This is where Linde Material Handling comes in. With our wide range of innovative safety solutions, we offer the ideal technology for any application, providing optimal protection for your drivers and employees as well as the transported goods, racking, and other machinery. From the initial safety analysis to sophisticated light solutions and intelligent assistance systems through to fleet management, we provide you with comprehensive support to help you implement safe and efficient intralogistics processes.

    Safety Consulting

    The Goal: Accident-free Intralogistics

    With our “Vision Zero” initiative, we at Linde Material Handling have set ourselves the ambitious goal of reducing the number of accidents at our customers’ premises to zero. This is based on our holistic approach to safety, which aims to maximize operational safety in intralogistics and eliminate accidents altogether. To help make this vision a reality, we are developing innovative safety features for Linde trucks as well as offering comprehensive safety consulting services.

    Safety Solutions Tailored to Your Warehouse

    The first step towards improved safety is often a professional analysis of the current situation. Our safety experts will systematically identify hazard points in your warehouse environment and develop a customized safety concept for you, including specific recommended actions for your company.     Linde Safety Guard

    In modern intralogistics systems people and industrial trucks often work side by side, in some cases under enormous time pressure. The risk of accidents is particularly high in areas where pedestrians and trucks frequently come into contact. Blind spots, areas with poor visibility, and high noise levels further increase the risk of accidents. Our Linde Safety Guard assistance system is a reliable solution to help increase the safety of drivers and pedestrians in the immediate vicinity of the truck. Warning zones are created around people, trucks, and infrastructure elements. In the event of danger, visual and acoustic warnings are emitted to warn those nearby. The warning zones can be individually adjusted.

    More about Linde Safety Guard

    Digital Fleet Management

    Our myLinde fleet management solution is packed with features that have been specially developed for safety in day-to-day warehouse operations. It can be used to set a speed limit for trucks, for example. myLinde also offers functions such as monitored access control, automatic transmission of error codes, and analysis of usage data.

    Eliminating the Risk of Explosions

    Explosive environments are a common occurrence in many industries, such as chemicals, pharmaceuticals, food, logistics, and recycling. In environments like this, gases, vapors, and dust can combine to create hazardous conditions. With this in mind, it is essential that industrial trucks used in these areas have the highest possible explosion protection.

    Linde Material Handling GmbH provides tailored explosion protection solutions that guarantee an adequate level of safety without compromising on efficiency and ergonomics. The portfolio includes explosion-proof versions of various counterbalanced forklift trucks, reach trucks, pallet stackers, and pallet trucks, as well as a range of safety systems with an explosion-proof design that comply with EU standard EN 1755 and the ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU. This enables Linde to ensure safe and efficient material flows, even under challenging, explosion-proof conditions.
