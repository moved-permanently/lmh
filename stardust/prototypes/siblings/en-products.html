    Linde forklift trucks and warehouse trucks

    Our Product Portfolio

    Always the right product for you

    With 77 product lines, Linde Material Handling offers customers the broadest range of intralogistics products. With the different chassis and mast variants, battery sizes, designs and cabins, as well as a large amount of special equipment available, the products are always configured to meet the respective requirements.

    You can find an overview of the product groups here:

    E-Trucks

    Linde Material Handling electric forklift trucks are ideal for precise and efficient logistics processes thanks to their special compact axle.

    Find out more

    Diesel Forklifts

    When it comes to diesel forklifts, Linde sets the benchmarks for productivity and efficiency. Load capacity from 1.4 to 8 tonnes: There is a Linde diesel forklift for every scenario.

    Find out more

    Gas Forklifts

    Load capacity from 1.4 to 8 tonnes: Gas forklifts from Linde Material Handling are the perfect embodiment of performance and efficiency, for every purpose.

    Find out more

    Heavy trucks

    Up to 18 ton lifting capacity: The robust heavy trucks from Linde Material Handling perform excellently even in particularly tough conditions.

    Find out more

    Pallet Trucks

    Pallet trucks from Linde Material Handling ensure rapid transport processes in the warehouse, for truck deliveries or order picking.

    Find out more

    Hand pallet trucks

    Linde Material Handling offers a wide selection of hand pallet trucks, ranging from its standard design to specialist pallet truck models with weighing scales and scissor lifts. Whatever option you choose, these versatile pieces of kit are a must for any warehouse.

    Find out more

    Pallet stackers

    Linde Material Handling pallet stackers lift loads of up to 2 tons at heights of up to 6 meters – 100% safely and precisely, even in the tightest of spaces.

    Find out more

    Very narrow aisle trucks

    High handling performance and particularly safe handling processes in narrow aisles - Linde Material Handling has designed its turret trucks and vertical order pickers to meet the challenges of very narrow aisles.

    Find out more

    Reach Trucks

    Load handling in warehouses requires numerous movements and maneuvering, often in confined spaces and at height. Linde reach trucks are tailor-made for these conditions.

    Find out more

    Order Pickers

    With their special design concept, Linde order pickers ensure fast, precise, and comfortable picking processes.

    Find out more

    Tow tractors and platform trucks

    The tow tractors and platform trucks from Linde Material Handling provide rapid and reliable transport, even with little space and in narrow aisles.

    Find out more

    Tugger Trains

    Powerful, versatile, and safe: Linde Material Handling has the right tugger train for every task, whether indoors or outdoors.

    Find out more

    Automated industrial trucks

    Linde Material Handling offers a wide range of automated industrial trucks for flexible and scalable automation solutions. The electric forklifts, pallet trucks, and tow tractors from the MATIC series are equipped with intelligent navigation and safety technology, and work together with people in the tightest of spaces.

    Find out more

    Explosion-Protected Trucks

    The explosion-proof trucks from Linde Material Handling combine the tried-and-tested advantages of series-produced trucks with comprehensive explosion protection measures as per the ATEX Product Directives.

    Find out more
