    FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions about Forklift Trucks

    What Is a Forklift Truck?

    A forklift truck is a motorized industrial truck and an indispensable piece of equipment in modern logistics and industry. It is used to transport and handle goods within warehouses, factories, and logistics centers. The truck consists of a motorized base frame with two adjustable fork arms that enable loads such as pallets, crates, and containers to be lifted, moved, and stacked. Forklift trucks are typically operated with different types of drive, from diesel and gas to electric. They vary in size and load capacity, from small models for light loads to large industrial trucks that can move loads up to several tons.

    Technically speaking, the term “forklift truck” refers specifically to counterbalanced forklift trucks. These are industrial trucks with forks and a counterweight at the rear.

    More broadly or colloquially, however, “forklift truck” often also includes warehouse trucks. Warehouse trucks are smaller trucks used for optimizing warehouse processes such as storage, transport, and order picking.
    Don’t forget: you can also purchase warehouse trucks online from the LindeShop.

    What Types of Forklift Truck Are There?

    Classic forklift trucks, also known as counterbalanced forklift trucks, are just one of several variants of modern industrial trucks. Counterbalanced forklift trucks pick up the load using front-mounted fork arms and have an integrated counterweight to stabilize the raised load. There are also other specialized forklift types such as rough-terrain forklifts, sideloaders, reach trucks, four-way forklifts, and multi-directional forklifts. Each of these variants is optimized for specific applications and tasks.

    Where Are Forklift Trucks Used?

    Forklift trucks are the all-rounders of the intralogistics sector. Whether in warehouses, factories, or outdoor yards, the maneuverable industrial trucks are used wherever goods need to be handled and transported. Typical areas of application include logistics, the manufacturing industry, and retail. However, forklift trucks also play a vital role in internal material flows in specialist fields such as the chemical, pharmaceutical, and oil and gas sectors. Thanks to their versatility and performance, they are indispensable tools in modern industry.

    How Much Does a Forklift Lift?

    The rated load capacity of a forklift truck is a decisive criterion when choosing the right model. Most forklifts can lift and transport loads between 1 and 8 metric tons. For particularly demanding applications, Linde Material Handling also offers heavy-duty forklift trucks with a load capacity of up to 18 metric tons. It is therefore important to check exactly what loads actually need to be moved in day-to-day work before purchasing a new forklift truck. That way, you can choose the right truck to reliably handle the required load capacity while remaining cost-efficient.

    What Is the Lifting Height of a Forklift Truck?

    The lifting height determines the height at which loads can be lifted and stored on racks. The lifting height of forklift trucks varies depending on the model and design, but is typically between 3 and 8 meters. The lifting height depends on the number and type of masts. Forklift trucks can be equipped with single, double, triple, or even quadruple masts, which influences the maximum lifting height. For specialist applications, Linde MH also offers custom versions with extra-high lifting heights.

    How Fast Can a Forklift Truck Travel?

    The maximum possible speed of forklift trucks varies depending on the model and drive type. On public roads, forklifts are generally limited to 25 km/h—at higher speeds, a standard driver’s license is required in addition to the forklift license. To prevent accidents, Linde Material Handling offers intelligent solutions for speed control. For example, the Linde Speed Assist system automatically adjusts the maximum speed to the truck’s surroundings. State-of-the-art radar sensor technology enables quick and easy adjustment to specific operating conditions and customer requirements—both indoors and outdoors.

    How Much Does a Forklift Truck Cost?

    The cost of a forklift truck depends on various factors, including the drive technology, load capacity, and motor or engine power of the truck. The optional equipment fitted to the truck also affects its price. To offer customers maximum flexibility, Linde MH has various financing options.
