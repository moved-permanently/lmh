    Linde Material Handling at LogiMAT 2026

    These were the Highlights

    At LogiMAT 2026, Linde Material Handling demonstrated what modern intralogistics looks like—tailored precisely to your needs and your success.

    Under the trade fair motto "Composed around you – Focused on your success," visitors experienced how mobile and stationary automation, digital solutions, modern energy concepts, and innovative vehicles come together to form a perfectly orchestrated overall system.

    This was LogiMAT 2026

    1 / 6

    Your intralogistics. Perfectly orchestrated.

    At the trade fair Linde Material Handling demonstrated how automation, software, trucks, and energy solutions integrate into a modular complete system—precisely tailored to your processes, your industry, and your company size.

    The result: intralogistics that grow with your requirements, stabilize processes, and make optimization potential transparent.

    Automation – Composed around you

    It became clear at LogiMAT: Your processes set the pace. Linde Material Handling combines mobile and stationary systems with intelligent software to create an automation solution that fits seamlessly into existing structures and can be flexibly expanded as needed.

    This creates end-to-end automation that not only speeds up processes, but also makes your entire material flow more transparent, stable, and predictable.

    Your advantages

    • Noticeably more efficient processes and higher throughput
    • Better space utilization and predictable total cost of ownership
    • Greater safety thanks to assistance systems and RTLS+
    • Scalable system architecture – from the first step to full automation
    • KPI-based control via myLinde and the Linde Warehouse Manager

    Mobile systems – flexible in material flow

    Standardized AGVs and AMRs handle transport, transfer, and storage and retrieval processes—quick to integrate, reliable in operation, and modularly expandable, for example with the L-MATIC core.

    Learn more

    Stationary systems – compact and powerful

    Shuttle and shelving systems such as the S-MATIC cube create high storage density and enable high turnover in a minimal space, ideal for dynamic and growing warehouse structures.

    Learn more

    Software & orchestration – networked, transparent, AI-supported

    The WMS Linde Warehouse Manager controls material flows holistically across all levels. myLinde bundles fleet, energy, and safety data, RTLS+ provides pinpoint real-time positions, and the digital twin (NVIDIA) accelerates the planning and simulation of your systems.

    Learn more

    Truck highlights at LogiMAT 2026

    Linde Xi Roadster – maximum visibility, maximum control

    The Linde Xi Roadster attracted a lot of attention at LogiMAT: It offers an exceptionally unobstructed field of vision—made possible by the elimination of the A-pillar. The result: precise, fast, and safe operation, both in narrow racking areas and in demanding outdoor applications.

    The robust construction, ergonomic operating concept, and modern energy options create a driving experience that impresses—from the first to the last use.

    Highlights:

    • All-round visibility for fast, controlled work
    • Ergonomic operating concept for high driver comfort
    • Attractive total cost of ownership thanks to modern energy options

    More about the Linde Xi Roadster

    Reach trucks Ri14 – Ri18 – compact, connected, efficient

    The new Ri14 to Ri18 generation of reach trucks showed how powerful compact warehouse technology is today. Load capacities from 1.4 to 1.8 tons, a compact chassis, lithium-ion battery, and a networked vehicle architecture ensure reliable performance, even in very narrow aisles.

    Highlights:

    • Compact L2 dimension for narrow aisles
    • Excellent all-round visibility
    • Standard connectivity for operational and diagnostic data

    Digital solutions, safety, and energy—for intralogistics that perform reliably

    Digital solutions – myLinde, RTLS+, and Digital Twin

    Transparency at the touch of a button: myLinde brings fleet, safety, and energy data together in a clear dashboard. RTLS+ shows every vehicle in real time—for full process visibility. The digital twin (NVIDIA) enables fast simulations and targeted process optimizations.

    More about myLinde

    Safety – smarter, safer, more transparent

    Ergonomic concepts, intelligent assistance systems, and precisely engineered sightlines significantly reduce risks—both indoors and in dynamic outdoor environments.

    More about safety solutions

    Energy – maximum availability, predictable costs

    With lithium-ion technology, the connect:charger, and intelligent energy management, you can increase the operational readiness of your fleet and ensure cost-effective operating processes.

    More about energy solutions
