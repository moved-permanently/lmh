    Linde MH at GSE Expo Europe 2026

    Keeping Pace with the Aviation Industry
    Come visit us at Booth H25 from September 15 to 17!
    • Trade Show Highlights
    • Advantages
    • Solutions

    Every day, the period between the cargo gate and the aircraft determines how punctually and efficiently the aviation industry operates. At the same time, the pressure on ground staff is growing noticeably: The industry expects to set a new record in 2026 with approximately 5.2 billion passengers worldwide. By 2050, global passenger demand is expected to more than double, while cargo volume will rise in parallel.

    This means shorter handling cycles, more pieces of baggage per hour, and more cargo units in the same space. In addition, qualified personnel are becoming scarcer and safety requirements are increasing. As a result, ground handling is taking center stage. It directly determines the on-time performance, service quality, and profitability of entire airlines. Reliable equipment plays a key role in ensuring the stability of the entire process chain.

    Anyone who wants to remain successful in this environment needs a partner who is thoroughly familiar with the key processes. To this end, Linde Material Handling combines agile vehicles, intelligent assistance systems, digital fleet management, and expert advice into a cohesive, comprehensive solution.

    Our Trade Show Highlights: Four Solutions for Airport Logistics

    P250 Automated Tow Tractor

    Where transport routes are repetitive and personnel resources are limited, the automation of towing tasks offers great potential. The P250 automated tow tractor moves luggage and heavy cargo with a towing capacity of up to 25 metric tons along predefined routes. A laser scanner integrated into the vehicle handles navigation within the environment. The automation solution was developed in collaboration with technology partner Götting KG.

    Xi Roadster Electric Counterbalanced Forklift

    In cargo terminals, warehouses, and on the tarmac, visibility and quick response times are key to safe and efficient operations. This is exactly where the Linde Xi Roadster shines. Whether loading and unloading aircraft or transporting palletized goods, the Roadster’s distinctive Linde design—featuring overhead tilt cylinders—eliminates the need for an A-pillar, giving the operator virtually unrestricted all-around visibility. This ensures that the path, load, and surroundings remain in full view at all times, even when handling bulky cargo units. The permanently integrated lithium-ion battery creates additional space in the cab. The Linde combination steering axle ensures high maneuverability and stability.

    Learn more

    W20 Electric Platform Truck with Luggage Trailer

    Every second counts when loading between terminals, baggage halls, and aircraft. With a two-metric-ton payload and a top speed of up to 20 km/h, the W20 platform truck reliably transports large volumes of baggage to their destination. A driver’s cab decoupled from the chassis and a drive axle with coil springs effectively shield the driver from shocks and vibrations, while the precise steering allows for effortless maneuvering in the tightest of spaces. Two AC drive motors provide the power needed for fast and reliable transport, powered either by a lithium-ion or lead-acid battery.

    Learn more

    myLinde

    In airport operations, the availability of each individual vehicle determines the punctuality of entire handling processes. The myLinde fleet management system combines administration, usage monitoring, safety, and energy management in a cloud-based portal and provides transparent data on usage, service, and utilization of the entire fleet. Thanks to its modular design, myLinde adapts flexibly to the requirements of international hubs.

    Learn more

    Our Safety and Energy Solutions

    Safety: Protection for People, Vehicles, and Processes

    Confined spaces, heavy traffic, and constant time pressure make the apron and the cargo terminal challenging work environments. With its “Vision Zero” initiative, Linde MH aims to completely eliminate accidents in intralogistics. The Linde Safety Guard demonstrates how this goal is put into practice: The system establishes warning zones around people, vehicles, and parts of the infrastructure. When an object approaches, those involved receive a visual and audible signal.

    Discover Safety Solutions

    Energy: Solutions for 24/7 Operations

    In airport operations, fleet size, network infrastructure, and workflows vary from location to location. There is no one-size-fits-all solution for energy systems. That’s why Linde Material Handling offers a comprehensive portfolio of drive systems, batteries, chargers, and software tools. Whether it’s a traditional internal combustion engine, a lead-acid battery, a lithium-ion powertrain, or a fuel cell: through expert consultation, we develop a customized energy concept for every location.
